Staff

    Classes

      Students

        Subjects

        Aggregate uses the first 4 subjects in this order, plus the 2 best grades from the remaining subjects.

          Leave both scores blank for a subject a student does not take — it will be skipped on their report.

          Attendance

          Record daily student attendance as Present, Absent, or Late. Late is counted as present in the term total. Holidays and Midterm days are excluded from attendance and Times Open. The attendance total is automatically reflected on report cards.

          Head Teacher only. Record teacher attendance as Present, Absent, Late, Excused, or On Leave. Holidays and Midterm days are excluded from attendance and Times Open. Teacher attendance is separate from student attendance.

          Grade Entry

          Standard layout: Class Score is out of 60, scaled to 50; Exam Score is out of 100, scaled to 50; Total is out of 100. Simple layout: only Exam Score (out of 100) is entered and used as the Total — Class Score is hidden.

          Bulk Grade (Excel)

          Export the current class's grade sheet, fill it in offline in Excel, then import it back — this replaces all scores for this class, term and year with what's in the file.

            Term History

              Start New Term

              Classes, students and subjects are shared across every term — nothing needs to be re-entered. This just moves the app's current Term/Year forward for new grade entry; every past term's data stays saved and browsable above.

              Activity Log

              A record of important changes made in this school. The Head Teacher can see school-wide activity; teachers see their own activity.

                Manage Teachers

                Approve pending requests (assigning their classes as you go), or disable/reactivate a teacher already in the school. Disabling never deletes their data — it just blocks their access.
