Help & FAQ

How can we help?

Quick answers for managing your school, entering grades, and keeping your records safe.

Getting started

What is AlatiphA SchoolHub?
SchoolHub is a school-management app for classes, students, subjects, staff, attendance, grades, remarks, reports, and backups.
How do I create or join a school?
After signing in, a Head Teacher can choose Create School. Teachers should choose Join School and enter the join code supplied by the Head Teacher. New teacher requests must be approved before access is granted.
What is the difference between Head Teacher and Teacher access?
The Head Teacher manages the school profile, staff, classes, subjects, teacher approvals, and assignments. Teachers see only the classes and subjects assigned to them.

Signing in

Can I use Google to sign in?
Yes. Select Continue with Google on the sign-in page and choose the same Gmail address linked to your SchoolHub account. Choosing a different Google account creates a separate account and does not move your existing school data.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Enter your email address on the sign-in page, then choose Forgot password?. Firebase will send a password-reset message to that email address.
Can I use SchoolHub without an account?
Yes. Continue without an account keeps data only on the current device. It does not create a shared school or cloud backup.

School records

How do I add classes, students, and staff?
Use the Classes, Students, and Staff pages. Each page has an Add section. A class can be linked to a Class Teacher after staff records have been created.
How do subjects work?
The Head Teacher manages the school subject list. Leave a student’s score blank for a subject they do not take; that subject is skipped on that student’s report. Subject order also determines the core-subject order used in aggregates.
How do I prevent duplicate subjects?
Use the existing subject entry rather than creating another with the same name. SchoolHub prevents duplicate names and keeps scores attached to the original subject.

Grades and reports

How do I enter grades?
Open Grades, select a class, enter Class Score and Exam Score, then choose Save Grades. In Simple layout, only the Exam Score is used.
Can I work with grades in Excel?
Yes. In the Grades page, export the current class sheet to Excel, fill it in, then import it back. Importing replaces grades for that class, term, and year, so export a backup first.
How do I create report cards?
Add grades and remarks, then open Reports. Select the class, review results, and generate an individual or class report. The Head Teacher can choose a report-card theme in Setup or Reports.

Attendance and terms

How do I record attendance?
Open Attendance, select the class and date, mark each student, then save. Head Teachers can also record teacher attendance and school-calendar events.
How do I start a new term?
Open Term History, choose the new term and academic year, then select Start New Term. Classes, students, and subjects carry over; grade entry starts fresh for the new term.

Backup and troubleshooting

How do I back up my school data?
Go to Setup → Backup & Restore → Export Backup (.json). Keep the downloaded file somewhere safe. Importing a backup replaces the current records, so export a fresh copy first.
The app looks outdated or is not responding.
Close and reopen SchoolHub. From the account menu, choose About SchoolHub → Check for Updates. If cloud records are delayed, use Sync Center in the account menu.
Why can’t a teacher see a class or subject?
The Head Teacher must approve the teacher and assign the appropriate classes and subjects in Manage Teachers.
