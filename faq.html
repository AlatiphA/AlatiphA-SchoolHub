Help & FAQ

Current SchoolHub help · Updated 4 October 2026

Use this guide for offline saves, account security, FIS, stores and multiple stock request drafts. Check whether a change is saved locally, kept as a draft or confirmed by the server.

Getting started & accounts

What is AlatiphA SchoolHub?

SchoolHub manages school setup, staff, classes, subjects, students, attendance, grades, remarks, reports, Fees & Receipts (FIS), stores and inventory, stock requests, assets, liabilities, notifications, backups and academic-year rollover.

What are my sign-in options?

The login page keeps email/password Sign in and Sign up first. Continue with Google appears below those options. Use the same account each time so SchoolHub returns you to the correct identity.

Does Continue with Google automatically join a school?

No. Google sign-in only authenticates the person. A Teacher must still choose to join an existing school, enter the school's join code, wait for Head Teacher approval, and receive class/subject assignments before entering the school workspace.

Why can a Google user appear in Firebase Authentication but not in Manage Teachers?

Firebase Authentication confirms that the Google account signed in successfully. The account appears in Manage Teachers only after the user completes SchoolHub's join-school process with the correct join code so a pending school membership is created.

How do I create or join a school?

Sign in first. A Head Teacher can register a new school and receives a join code. A Teacher joins an existing school with that code, then waits for the Head Teacher to approve the request and assign classes/subjects.

What can a Teacher access?

An approved Teacher works with assigned classes, pupils and subjects. Teachers can edit their linked personal record in Profile → My Details, view the stock catalogue and submit their own stock requests. Manage Teachers, school setup, staff administration, financial changes, inventory management, assets and liabilities are managed by the Head Teacher.

Can I use SchoolHub without an account?

Yes, through the 7-day guest trial. Guest data stays on the current device and is not a shared cloud school. Export a backup before clearing browser/app data.

Fees & Receipts (FIS)

What is the current Fees & Receipts system?

FIS is SchoolHub's multi-category fee-item system. It keeps reusable fee categories, term/year fee items, pupil charges, payments, allocations, balances, receipts, adjustments, arrears and financial reports while preserving migrated legacy v40 fee records.

What are fee categories?

Fee categories are reusable labels for groups of charges, such as PTA, Feeding, Examination, Sports or Development. A category can be reused across different fee items and academic periods.

Who can a fee item be applied to?

A fee item can be applied to one class, multiple selected classes, all classes, or one individual pupil. This lets a school keep several separate fee items in the same term.

Who can make financial changes?

Protected FIS financial mutations are restricted to the active Head Teacher. The system keeps financial writes behind the server-backed safeguards used by the current ledger.

Pupil charges, payments & receipts

What do Due, Paid and Balance mean?

Each pupil charge keeps separate values for Due, Paid and Balance, so SchoolHub does not have to treat a pupil's fees as one undifferentiated amount.

How are payments allocated?

Automatic allocation clears the oldest outstanding debt first. Manual allocation is also available when a payment must be directed to selected charges.

What information appears on a receipt?

Receipts show the payment and its allocation lines so you can trace which fee items were settled by that payment. Void status remains visible when a payment has been reversed.

How do discounts and adjustments work?

Individual discounts and other adjustments can change what is due without destroying the original charge. Safe-edit rules prevent a charge from being reduced below money already paid.

What is a payment void or reversal?

A reversal is the controlled way to undo a payment. It restores the affected balance through the ledger instead of simply deleting evidence that money was previously recorded.

Financial reports, statements & arrears

What does the Financial Dashboard show?

The dashboard can show Total Expected, Total Collected, Outstanding, Collection Rate, Arrears, Pupils Owing, Discounts and Added Adjustments from confirmed FIS records.

How can financial reports be filtered?

Reports can be filtered by academic year, term, class, fee category, balance status and production/test records. Reporting can also group information by category, class, academic period and payment method.

Can I export or print financial reports?

Yes. Detailed financial data can be exported to CSV, and report views can be printed or saved as PDF using the browser's print system.

What is a Pupil Fee Statement?

A pupil statement brings together fee items, categories, academic periods, base charges, adjustments, due, paid, balance, receipts, payment methods, references and void status where applicable.

What is a Parent Fee Statement?

The parent-facing statement uses the pupil's saved Guardian Name and Parent Phone details and separates previous arrears from current outstanding. Future charges are excluded by default unless they are deliberately included.

What does Arrears Management do?

Arrears Management separates previous-period arrears from current-term outstanding balances and supports class, fee-category, guardian/phone and production/test filtering. Settled, cancelled, future and test records are excluded from the normal production arrears view by default.

Reminder Generator

Who can I prepare a fee reminder for?

You can prepare a reminder for one pupil or for selected owing pupils from Arrears Management. When using Selected arrears pupils, first tick the pupils in the arrears table.

What can a reminder cover?

A reminder can cover previous arrears only, or previous arrears plus the current-term outstanding balance. You can also add an optional deadline and note.

What does Copy message do?

It copies the prepared reminder to the device clipboard. After a successful copy, SchoolHub shows visible feedback so you know the reminder was copied.

What does Open WhatsApp do?

It opens the prepared reminder in the WhatsApp app or the browser destination available on the device. SchoolHub does not claim that the message was sent; you still decide whether to send it.

What does Print / Save reminder PDF do?

It opens the printable reminder so you can print it or use the browser/device print system to save it as PDF.

Why does the Reminder Generator say there is no eligible balance?

Parent reminders use eligible production balances, not test fee records. Also check that the reminder covers the correct period and that the intended pupil or selected arrears pupils actually have an outstanding eligible balance.

FIS pending saves & confirmed balances

What does a pending FIS save mean?

It means a financial action is waiting for the server to accept it. Pending financial entries are deliberately excluded from confirmed balances until the server confirms them.

How do I know a fee or stock save is confirmed?

Check the specific fee or stock action as well as the header. A queued action is not confirmed until its server confirmation succeeds. The header counts supported school-record and stock uploads. Fees also have their own pending-save list, which you should check even when the header shows Up to date. However, Up to date does not mean that an unsent form draft has been submitted or that another device has already refreshed its display. Review pending saves and errors before recording the action again.

What happens if an FIS queue entry is rejected?

A rejected financial entry can block later financial saves until it is reviewed. This prevents SchoolHub from silently skipping a failed financial operation and continuing as though nothing happened.

What does Dismiss rejected entry do?

It removes the rejected queue item itself. It does not delete an already confirmed fee, payment, receipt or pupil charge. After resolving the queue, use Refresh balances when needed to reload confirmed ledger information.

Offline mode, Background Sync & PWA updates

Can I change my login email and password in Profile?

Open Profile → Account Security. Email/password accounts can change their password after confirming the current password, or request a verification link for a new login email. Verify the link before using the new address. Your account, school records and Staff link are preserved. Google-only accounts manage their login credentials in Google. The contact email in My Details remains separate.

Why do qualifications use dropdowns in My Details?

Teachers and Head Teachers use the same academic and professional qualification choices. Choose Other when none applies. Older nonstandard values remain visible until corrected and are not erased when another detail is edited.

Can SchoolHub work offline?

Yes, after this device has loaded the school workspace for a verified signed-in account. Previously loaded records remain available offline. First sign-in, joining a school and loading data on a new device require internet. Offline access is limited to the records and permissions already available on this device.

What does Sync Pending mean?

A supported record change is saved on this device and waiting for upload. It survives closing and reopening when local storage is available. Several changes to one pupil can still count as 1 pending; the count is not the number of fields you edited. Reconnect and keep the app open until verification and upload finish. A saved form draft is separate from the upload queue.

Does SchoolHub use Background Sync?

Yes, where the browser supports the native Background Sync API. It works with SchoolHub's existing pending queues rather than replacing them. If native Background Sync is unavailable, reconnect/reopen recovery still remains available.

What happens if the PWA is completely closed?

Saved edits and drafts remain on the same device. Supported browsers may upload queued records through Background Sync after the window closes, but timing is not guaranteed. Reopen SchoolHub with internet and wait for confirmation if a save remains pending. My Details and stock request drafts are not submitted automatically. Signing out stops background uploads.

How are service-worker updates handled?

The current PWA manages service-worker registration, update checks and lifecycle events more robustly and reports registration problems through diagnostics instead of silently ignoring them.

What should I do if Sync Pending does not clear?

Keep the app open with internet, then open Home → Settings → Sync Center and use Sync Now. Read any named record, permission or conflict error before retrying. For a pending fee or stock transaction, retry the retained save rather than entering a new copy. Do not clear browser/app data while edits or drafts need recovery.

Account security & My Details

Do I need to verify my signup email?

New Email/Password accounts receive a verification email after signup. Open the link and choose I have verified my email before creating or joining a school. Use Resend verification email if necessary and check Spam/Junk. Teachers still need Head Teacher approval. Existing active school members retain access during this transition; pending members must verify before approval.

Can I save My Details offline?

While online, open Profile → My Details and save a draft before working offline. Edits are saved as a draft on this device for your account and school. Reopen My Details offline to continue. Reconnect, wait for verification, reopen the form and press Save My Details. Only Confirmed by the server means the change has been accepted. School-managed fields remain read only.

What if my Staff link or personal details changed elsewhere?

SchoolHub checks the linked Staff record and the original values when you submit. A changed link or conflicting edit may block the save. Keep the draft, read the message and contact the Head Teacher if your Staff link needs correction. A local draft does not replace a confirmed server record.

Do login changes work offline?

No. Use Profile → Account Security while connected. Password changes require the current password. A new login email requires verification at the new address before it becomes your login address. Passwords are not saved as drafts. Google-only accounts manage credentials through Google. Updating the contact email in My Details does not change your login email.

Why is a second account with my email blocked?

SchoolHub checks for another membership using the same email in the school. Keep using your approved account and ask the Head Teacher to review duplicate accounts rather than joining again through a different sign-in method.

Weekly Supervision

What does Weekly Supervision record?

The Head Teacher records lesson-plan submission and review, weekly exercise and assessment counts by subject, books checked and marked, attendance-register checks, feedback and follow-up. These are supervision observations, not automatically calculated pupil grades or daily staff attendance. Subject choices come from the Subjects tab in its saved order, with repeated names shown once. A subject already selected in another row is unavailable until that row is changed or removed. The Head Teacher is the reviewer and is excluded from new teacher checks; existing historical checks remain available. Who can see or change weekly supervision?

The Head Teacher records and edits checks. Teachers can view only checks linked to their own Staff record; an unlinked account must ask the Head Teacher to link it. Teachers cannot change supervision outcomes.

Can I prepare supervision checks offline?

Yes, if the verified workspace and staff choices are already cached. Entries remain a draft on this device. Use Resume saved draft after reopening. Saving an official check requires internet access and a verified account. If confirmation fails, retry the same retained save. Use Refresh checks to see another device's changes.

Stores, Assets & Liabilities

Where do I find Stores, Assets & Liabilities?

Open it from Home, after Reports. The Head Teacher manages stores and inventory, stock requests, assets, liabilities and history. Teachers see the stock catalogue and My Requests.

How do I edit a record without losing my place?

Use Edit beside a Staff, Student, Class or Subject record to open its editing window. Staff and Student detail windows also have an Edit button. Change the fields and choose Update, or use Close to leave without saving those field changes. Background refreshes keep your entered values. If the record changes elsewhere, close and reopen it to review the latest details before updating. Your account permissions still apply.

Do I need to enter a stock code?

No. Add stock item assigns a code automatically when the server confirms the save: SC-001, SC-002, and so on. Existing codes stay unchanged. Reserved codes are not reused, so gaps can occur. Asset tags are still entered manually.

How do I find an item or view its details?

Use Search this section to find codes, item names and other displayed details. Swipe sideways inside wide tables; headings and the first column stay visible. View opens the record details. The Head Teacher can use the available Edit or stock actions from there.

What do Receive stock, Issue stock and Correct stock do?

Receive records a delivery, Issue records stock given out, and Correct records a signed quantity adjustment. Enter the quantity and reference or reason. These actions require a verified online Head Teacher and server confirmation. Stock quantities support up to three decimal places. An asset records school property; a liability records a supplier bill or debt. Recording a liability payment does not transfer money.

Can I prepare requests for several items offline?

Yes. Load the stock catalogue online first. Choose one item, quantity and purpose, then tap Save draft & add another. Prepare the next item and save it. Each item has its own draft; a new request does not replace the earlier ones.

Where do my saved stock request drafts appear?

Open My Requests → Saved request drafts. Use Open draft to check or edit one. Drafts survive reopening on this device and stay separate for each account and school. Editing or discarding one leaves the others unchanged. Drafts are not shared to other devices.

How do I send saved stock requests?

Reconnect and wait for account verification. Open a draft and tap Send request. Submit each item separately. A confirmed request appears in My Requests and its draft is removed. Other drafts remain saved. If confirmation is delayed, use the retained pending request to retry; do not create another copy. Resolve a pending save before sending the next request.

Does requesting stock reserve or issue it?

No. An offline draft has not been submitted. A submitted request awaits the Head Teacher. Approve & issue issues the approved quantity once; Reject does not issue stock. Review the request status and decision note.

Why does the Stores tab update without a full page refresh?

While this section is visible, online and verified, it checks for updated requests and records about every 15 seconds. It pauses while a form is open or verification is unfinished. Records remain visible during refresh, and unchanged data does not redraw the tables. Search, the selected section and table scroll are preserved. Failed refreshes show saved records with an error message.

Offline saves, drafts & reconnecting

Which pupil and teaching edits can I save offline?

Supported pupil edits, offline pupil additions, grades, pupil attendance and remarks save locally before upload. Teachers remain restricted to assigned classes and subjects. Complete required fields and wait for the local save to finish. Keep the device data until the pending change has uploaded. Imports, deletions, staff administration and protected financial or stock actions may require internet.

What is the difference between saved locally, a draft and server-confirmed?

Saved locally / pending is a record change waiting in the upload queue. A draft is a form saved on this device that you must submit manually after reconnecting. Server-confirmed means the server accepted that action. My Details and stock request drafts do not upload merely because the header says Up to date.

Why does it say Waiting to reconnect when my data is on?

A network connection is detected, but SchoolHub has not yet verified your account and cloud connection. It retries about every 10 seconds while open. You may see Waiting to reconnect → Checking → Up to date. Pending edits stay saved while verification runs; data being switched on does not by itself confirm that an upload succeeded.

Will another device show my changes automatically?

An open Classes list receives supported class additions, renames and confirmed deletions automatically. The Stores section refreshes its own records while visible and verified. Other lists, including Students, may need a refresh on the observing device. To confirm an upload, check the saved value from the other signed-in account after refreshing that list.

Why do I sometimes see only the pupil I searched for?

Check the search box and clear its filter to show the whole class. Successful pupil additions clear the search filter. A filtered list does not mean the other pupils were deleted.

Mobile tables & horizontal scrolling

How do I view wide tables on a phone?

Swipe left or right inside the table to scroll horizontally. The page itself continues to scroll vertically as normal.

Why does the pupil/name column stay visible?

Wide Fees & Receipts tables pin their main row-label column so the pupil or record name remains visible while you scroll across the financial columns.

What is frozen in the Arrears table?

The selection checkbox and pupil/name area remain pinned while the rest of the arrears columns scroll horizontally.

Are table headings aligned with the values?

Yes. FIS table headings and their data are centered consistently so the columns are easier to follow on desktop and mobile.

Save, Update & deleting

What is the difference between Save and Update?

Save is used for a new entry. Update is used when editing an existing record such as Staff, Student, Class, Subject, Teacher assignment or Calendar event.

Why are some deletes blocked when offline?

Important destructive operations are designed to fail closed. Where SchoolHub needs cloud confirmation before deleting or restructuring records, reconnect first so local and cloud data cannot silently diverge.

How does Calendar editing work?

Edit loads the original calendar event. Updating the type/note changes that event. Changing its date moves it to the new date and removes the original entry rather than leaving a duplicate.

Staff, students & school records

How do I add staff, students, classes and subjects?

Use their pages from the dashboard. Add new records with Save. Use Edit, then Update, for existing records. Students and staff also support recovery checks from saved copies where available.

Where are staff signatures and student photos stored?

SchoolHub keeps image data out of normal Firestore record fields. Cloud image files are handled through Firebase Storage while Firestore keeps lightweight references/metadata. This avoids oversized Firestore document fields.

Can I import or export data with Excel?

Supported staff/grade workflows provide spreadsheet transfer options. Review imports carefully because an import can update multiple records at once. Keep a backup before major imports.

Attendance & Calendar

How do I record pupil attendance?

Open Attendance, choose the class and date, mark pupils and save. Date arrows let you move between days. Unmark All clears the current marks before saving.

What can the Head Teacher record?

Head Teachers can record pupil attendance, teacher attendance, and Calendar exceptions such as holidays and midterms.

Do Print and Download Report use the same attendance design?

Yes. The attendance summary/report download uses the same modern report layout used for printing.

Grades, remarks & reports

How do I enter grades?

Open Grades, choose a class, enter the required scores and save the grade sheet. Wide grade tables can scroll horizontally when many subjects are present and score inputs support full three-digit values.

How do reports work?

Add grades and remarks, then open Reports. Select the class/student and generate the available report. Themes can be previewed where enabled. Complete the subject results before issuing a final report; incomplete results can affect the subjects shown and aggregate.

Why is the aggregate a dash, or why are fewer subjects shown?

An aggregate is not calculated when fewer than six subject results are complete; the report shows a dash and no aggregate-based class position. Only completed results available for that report are listed. When results are missing, the report explains the subjects shown and the dash near the footer. Ask the school for clarification or an updated report.

Why is Billing & Credits sometimes unavailable?

Billing controls are Head Teacher-only. The current build can suspend live billing while updates are in progress, in which case SchoolHub shows that no payment or credit is required for report generation during the suspension.

Backup, recovery & rollover

How do I create a backup?

Go to Setup → Backup & Restore → Export Backup (.json). Store the downloaded JSON file somewhere safe.

Can SchoolHub recover missing students or staff?

Recovery tools can compare the current list with available browser snapshots/saved copies and cloud records. Recovery only works when the missing record exists in one of those sources.

How do I start a new term?

Use Term History to start the next term/year while preserving the school structure and historical records.

What is Academic Year Rollover?

At year end, the Head Teacher reviews each student as Promote, Repeat, Graduate/Complete, or Transfer/Leave. The rollover creates a year-end backup/archive and starts the new academic year at Term 1.

What is Restore Year-End Backup?

It is an emergency reversal tool for a SchoolHub academic-year rollover JSON. SchoolHub validates the backup and restores the backed-up roster/settings without unnecessarily deleting later grades, attendance, remarks, staff, classes, subjects or calendar data.

Notifications & communication

When does the Head Teacher receive notifications?

SchoolHub notifies the Head Teacher when a Teacher submits a school join request and when an existing school Teacher explicitly signs in. Refreshing the page or restoring an already signed-in session does not create a new sign-in notification.

What notifications can a Teacher receive?

Teachers receive account and assignment notices, school announcements and stock request decisions. The Head Teacher receives stock request notices as well as school join and explicit teacher sign-in notices. Use the bell icon to review the saved notification history.

Where do I see notifications?

Use the bell icon or Home → Settings → Notifications. Unread notifications show a badge. Pending Teachers can also open Notifications from the Waiting for Approval screen.

Can SchoolHub show browser or PWA notifications?

Yes, while SchoolHub is running or backgrounded on a supported device. Enable the option inside Notifications. In-app notifications remain available even if browser notification permission is not granted.

Can the Head Teacher send an announcement?

Yes. Open Notifications and use Send school announcement. The announcement is delivered to all active Teacher accounts and remains in each Teacher's notification history.

Do browser notifications work when SchoolHub is completely closed?

Not in Notifications v1. The persisted in-app notification will still be waiting the next time the user opens SchoolHub. Fully closed-app push delivery can be added later with Firebase Cloud Messaging web-push configuration.

Install, security & troubleshooting

Can I install SchoolHub like an app?

Yes. Use Home → Help & About → Install SchoolHub when installation is supported by your browser/device. The PWA caches the application shell for reliable reopening.

What is the PIN Lock?

The optional PIN Lock is a local privacy screen for a shared device. It does not replace your Firebase account password or the Head Teacher/Teacher permission system.

Where can I check the app status?

Use Home → Settings → System Health for service, storage, service-worker and data checks, and Home → Settings → Sync Center for cloud synchronization.

The app looks outdated. What should I do?

Reopen SchoolHub while online and use Home → Help & About → About SchoolHub or System Health to check the installed build. Refresh once if necessary after an update. Firebase Hosting and GitHub Pages can show different versions until both deployments are updated. Keep pending edits and drafts safe; clearing app data removes local copies.

Recommended workflow & key reminders

What is the recommended SchoolHub workflow?

Create the Head Teacher school account, complete Setup, add Staff, Classes, Subjects and Students, share the join code, approve Teachers and assign their classes/subjects, configure the Calendar, record Attendance, enter Grades and Remarks, generate Reports, create and manage FIS charges/payments where used, manage stores, stock requests, assets and liabilities, review financial reports/arrears/statements/reminders, monitor synchronization, export backups, start new terms when appropriate and use Academic Year Rollover at year end.

What should I remember most when using SchoolHub?

Keep records current, complete the school-joining and approval process carefully, use role permissions responsibly, monitor synchronization, treat only server-confirmed FIS balances as confirmed, review financial queue errors before dismissing them, and maintain regular exported backups.
