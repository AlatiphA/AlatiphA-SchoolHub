Chapter 2: SchoolHub Accounts

SchoolHub uses different account types because not every user should have access to every part of a school's information.

2.1 Guest Account

Guest Mode is designed for someone who wants to try SchoolHub before creating a school account.

The current application provides a 7-day guest trial.

In Guest Mode, you can experiment with supported areas such as School Setup, Classes, Subjects, Staff, Students, Attendance, Grades, Remarks and Reports.

Important: Guest information is stored on the current device rather than as a shared SchoolHub cloud school. Export a backup before clearing browser/app data, uninstalling the app or changing devices.

2.2 School / Head Teacher Account

The Head Teacher account is the main administrative account for a SchoolHub school.

A Head Teacher can manage School Settings, Staff, Classes, Subjects, Students, Teacher applications and approvals, Teacher assignments, Attendance, Calendar, Grades, Remarks, Reports, Weekly Supervision, Stores & Inventory, Stock Requests, Assets, Liabilities, Fees & Receipts, Financial Reports, Arrears, Parent Statements, Reminders, Term History, Academic Year Rollover, Backup and Recovery, System Health and Sync information.

After a school is created, teachers can join it using the school's join code. Their access does not automatically become active. The Head Teacher approves the teacher and determines the classes and subjects the teacher can use.

2.3 Teacher Account

A Teacher joins an existing SchoolHub school rather than creating another school.

  1. Create or sign in to a SchoolHub account.
  2. Join the required school using the school's join code.
  3. Wait for Head Teacher approval.
  4. The Head Teacher assigns permitted classes and subjects.
  5. The teacher works only within those approved assignments, can view the Stock Catalogue and submit their own stock requests, and can view Weekly Supervision checks linked to their Staff record.
If a teacher cannot see an expected class or subject, the Head Teacher should first check the teacher's assignment in Manage Teachers.

2.4 Sign in, Sign up, Google and Joining a School

New email/password signup: SchoolHub sends a verification email. Open the link in your inbox, return to SchoolHub and choose I have verified my email before creating or joining a school. Check Spam/Junk and the address you entered. Use Resend verification email after its waiting period if needed. Google sign-in supplies its verified email. Verification proves access to the email address; it does not replace Head Teacher approval. Existing active school accounts keep their access under the current transition safeguards.

The current login page keeps the main email/password workflow first. Users can sign in with an existing email/password account, switch to Sign Up to create an account, or use Continue with Google below those options.

Google sign-in is authentication, not automatic school membership. A person who signs in successfully with Google for the first time must still join an existing SchoolHub school with the correct join code, or create a new school if they are setting up their own school.

For a teacher joining an existing school, the recommended sequence is:

  1. Sign in with the account the teacher intends to keep using, or choose Continue with Google.
  2. Complete email verification if prompted, then choose to join an existing school.
  3. Enter the school's join code.
  4. SchoolHub sends the join request through a protected server-side membership check.
  5. If the authenticated email is not already being used by another pending, active or disabled account in that school, SchoolHub creates a pending teacher membership.
  6. The Head Teacher receives the join notification and sees the teacher in Manage Teachers.
  7. The Head Teacher approves the teacher and assigns permitted classes and subjects.
  8. The teacher receives an approval notification and can enter the school workspace.
One teacher, one school identity: SchoolHub prevents another Firebase UID with the same normalised email address from joining the same school as a second teacher membership. If SchoolHub reports that the email is already linked to another account, the teacher should sign in with the existing account or ask the Head Teacher to resolve the duplicate.
Google sign-in is not school approval. Seeing a Google account in Firebase Authentication confirms only that authentication succeeded. The user must still complete the SchoolHub joining process and receive Head Teacher approval.

2.5 Account Security

Active signed-in users can open Profile → Account Security. The login email is separate from the contact email stored in My Details.

  • Email/password accounts: can change the password after confirming the current password.
  • Login email: can be changed by requesting a verification link for the new address. The new address becomes the login address only after verification.
  • Google-only accounts: manage their Google email and password through Google Account Security.
Account Security requires internet. Passwords and credential changes are never placed in SchoolHub's offline queues or drafts. Changing the login email or password keeps the same SchoolHub UID, school membership and Staff link.

Chapter 3: Home Dashboard

The Home tab acts as the main starting point after entering SchoolHub. It provides quick access to the areas available to the current user's role. The top-bar notification bell shows an unread badge when new SchoolHub notifications are waiting.

Major areas include Setup, Manage Teachers, Staff, Classes, Subjects, Students, Attendance, Grades, Remarks, Fees & Receipts, Reports, Stores, Assets & Liabilities, Weekly Supervision, Activity Log and Term History, depending on the user's role. Billing & Credits remains a separate Head Teacher area when available.

The floating quick-navigation pill also provides fast access to frequently used sections such as Home, Attendance and Reports.

Chapter 4: School Setup

The Setup tab contains the main configuration for the school.

4.1 School Information

School settings include Teacher Name where applicable, School Name, Address, Email, School Logo, Current Term, Academic Year, Term Opening Date, Term Closing Date, Calculated Times Open, Next Term Begins and Head Teacher for report-card signatures.

4.2 Report Settings

SchoolHub supports two main report layouts:

The current build also contains eight report-card themes:

  1. Black & White
  2. SchoolHub Professional
  3. Modern Academic
  4. Classic Academic
  5. Executive
  6. Sage Minimal
  7. Blue Air
  8. Rose Editorial

4.3 Backup & Restore

SchoolHub can export a school backup in JSON format. The Export Backup (.json) option provides an additional copy of important school information outside the application.

4.4 PIN Lock

The optional PIN Lock provides an additional privacy screen on a device where SchoolHub may be accessible to other people.

The PIN Lock is a local privacy feature. It does not replace the user's Firebase account password or SchoolHub's Head Teacher/Teacher permissions.

4.5 Cloud Sync

The Cloud Sync section shows the connection and synchronisation state of the school workspace. When needed, Sync Now can be used to trigger synchronisation.

The current PWA also manages its service-worker lifecycle more robustly, checks for updates and supports native Background Sync where the browser provides it. SchoolHub still keeps its own local pending queues as the source of truth, so unsupported browsers can continue using reconnect and reopen recovery.

4.6 Academic Year Rollover

At the end of the academic year, SchoolHub provides a structured rollover process.

Each student can be reviewed as Promote, Repeat, Graduate / Complete, or Transfer / Leave.

SchoolHub creates a protective year-end backup/archive as part of the rollover process and provides a Restore Year-End Backup option for emergency reversal.

Chapter 5: Manage Teachers

The Manage Teachers tab is a Head Teacher administrative area used to review teacher requests, approve teachers, assign classes and subjects, update active assignments, disable or reactivate access and remove a teacher from the school when appropriate.

Teacher accounts and Staff records are related but separate. Approval and assignment controls determine what an authenticated teacher can access in the school workspace.

5.1 Account identity and duplicate protection

The current build shows the teacher account's full Firebase UID in Manage Teachers and can display a Duplicate email warning when more than one school membership uses the same normalised email address.

SchoolHub also performs duplicate checks on the server. A new join request, approval or reactivation is blocked if another pending, active or disabled membership in the same school already owns the same normalised email address.

Do not approve duplicate identities. If two entries represent the same person and email, identify the account the teacher should keep. Use SchoolHub's existing Remove action on the unwanted school membership after confirming the correct account and Staff link. Removing the SchoolHub membership first is safer than deleting a Firebase Authentication user first.

5.2 Teacher lifecycle actions

When the Head Teacher approves, rejects, disables, reactivates, removes or changes the class/subject assignments of a teacher, SchoolHub can create an in-app notification for the affected teacher. These notifications are explained in the Notifications & Communication chapter.

Chapter 6: Staff

The Staff tab keeps structured staff information such as name, sex, date of birth, Staff ID, registration/licence details, EMIS, SSNIT, Ghana Card ID, rank, dates, academic and professional qualifications, bank details, phone, email, role and signature.

Academic and professional qualifications use consistent choices across Staff and My Details. Other is available when the required qualification is not listed, while older saved values remain visible until deliberately corrected.

Tools include Staff Search, Bulk Add, Excel export, Excel template download, Staff spreadsheet import, Print / Save as PDF, and missing-staff recovery checks.

The Add Staff form preserves an unfinished device-scoped draft, including the selected signature where the browser can retain it. Reopening the form restores the draft, but the staff member has not been added until the normal save completes.

Editing an existing Staff record opens in a popup window. Staff detail views also provide an Edit action.

Chapter 7: Classes

The Classes tab is used to create and organise the school's classes and can link each class to a Class Teacher.

When the Classes list is already open on another verified device, supported class additions, renames and confirmed deletions can appear automatically without a full page refresh. If a class edit form is open, SchoolHub waits until that form is finished or closed before replacing the displayed list.

Editing an existing Class opens in a popup window so the user does not have to leave the current list position.

Chapter 8: Students

The Students tab manages pupil records such as Full Name, Sex, Date of Birth, Age, Student ID, Disability status, Guardian Name, Parent Phone, House Number / GhanaPost GPS and Class.

Students can be searched across the school, filtered by class, added individually or in bulk, and checked against saved recovery sources when records appear missing.

Supported pupil edits and pupil additions can be saved locally while offline after the device has a verified school workspace. They remain pending until the account is revalidated and the upload succeeds. A successful pupil addition clears the active pupil-search filter so the full class can be shown again.

Editing an existing Student opens in a popup window, and the Student details window also provides an Edit action.

Chapter 9: Subjects

The Subjects tab defines the subjects taught in the school. Users can add subjects, edit existing subjects and arrange subject order.

Subject order matters for report calculations and is also the display order used by Weekly Supervision. Editing an existing Subject opens in a popup window.

Chapter 10: Attendance

10.1 Student Attendance

Choose a class and date, mark the pupils and save the attendance record. Date navigation controls make it easier to move between attendance dates.

10.2 Teacher Attendance

Head Teachers can maintain teacher attendance separately from pupil attendance.

10.3 Calendar

The Calendar is used to manage school-day exceptions such as holidays and midterms. Editing an event updates the existing record, and changing its date moves it rather than creating a duplicate.

10.4 Summary

The Summary area provides consolidated attendance information for the selected period.

10.5 Analytics

The Analytics section helps turn recorded attendance into information that can be reviewed more easily than individual daily registers.

10.6 Attendance Reports

Attendance reports can be prepared for printing and downloading using a common modern report presentation.

Chapter 11: Grade Entry

The Grade Entry tab is where assessment scores are entered for pupils. It supports manual entry, saving class grades, exporting to Excel and importing from Excel.

On desktop and smaller screens, wide grade ledgers can scroll horizontally when many subjects are present, and grade inputs support full three-digit values.

Chapter 12: Remarks

The Remarks tab provides the non-score information used on pupil reports. Available actions include Save Remarks and Save & Next.

Chapter 13: Reports

13.1 Report Card Themes

Schools can select from the available report-card themes before producing reports.

13.2 PDF Reports

SchoolHub can prepare individual and class report-card PDFs. The current workflow includes visible generation progress and a Report ready stage.

  • Save PDF
  • Open / Print PDF

These actions use the already-prepared report rather than unnecessarily generating the same PDF again.

13.3 CSV Results

Results can also be exported in CSV format for spreadsheet work, record keeping or further analysis.

13.4 Aggregate and Report Completeness

The report aggregate uses six completed subject results: the first four subjects in the school's saved subject order plus the two best grades from the remaining completed subjects.

If fewer than six subject results are complete, SchoolHub does not calculate an aggregate. The report shows a dash for the aggregate and does not assign an aggregate-based class position. Only completed results available for that report are listed, and the report explains when the aggregate is unavailable because subjects are incomplete.

Chapter 14: Stores, Assets & Liabilities

The Stores, Assets & Liabilities area manages school stock, teacher stock requests, school property, supplier bills/debts and a recent confirmed management history. It appears on Home after Reports.

Head Teachers see Stores & Inventory, Stock Requests, Assets, Liabilities and History. Teachers see the Stock Catalogue and My Requests.

Stock items and assets use the following standard categories:

14.2 Stock Items and Stock Codes

The Head Teacher can add a stock item with its item name, category, unit, opening quantity, low-stock threshold and unit cost. SchoolHub generates the stock code automatically after server confirmation using codes such as SC-001, SC-002 and so on.

Reserved stock codes are not reused, so gaps can occur. Existing codes remain unchanged. Stock items can be searched, viewed and edited. An item can be archived only through the protected inventory workflow.

14.3 Receive, Issue and Correct Stock

The Head Teacher can record three protected stock movements:

  • Receive stock: records a delivery and its supplier/delivery reference.
  • Issue stock: records stock given out and the recipient or purpose.
  • Correct stock: records a signed quantity adjustment with a reason.

Stock quantities support up to three decimal places. These actions require a verified online Head Teacher and server confirmation. The dashboard can highlight low stock using the saved minimum threshold.

14.4 Teacher Stock Requests and Offline Drafts

Teachers can view active catalogue items and submit their own stock requests. A request contains the stock item, quantity and purpose. A submitted request does not reserve or issue stock while it is waiting.

The Head Teacher can Approve & issue a pending request, which issues the approved quantity once, or Reject it with a decision note. A teacher can cancel their own pending request. Stock-request decisions can also appear in SchoolHub notifications.

Teachers can prepare multiple request drafts on one device. Save draft & add another keeps each item as a separate device-scoped draft. Reconnect, wait for account verification, open each draft and choose Send request. Drafts are not shared automatically to other devices.

Pending requests: if a submission is waiting for server confirmation, retry the retained pending request instead of entering a second copy. Resolve the pending save before submitting another stock transaction.

14.5 Assets, Liabilities and History

The Assets register tracks school property by asset tag, name, category, acquisition cost, recorded value, purchase date, location, custodian, condition and status such as In use, Under repair or Disposed.

The Liabilities register tracks a supplier/creditor, description, invoice reference, due date, amount owed, amount paid and outstanding balance. Recording a liability payment updates the register; it does not transfer money. Recorded payments can be reversed through the protected history workflow when necessary.

The History tab shows the latest confirmed management events, including stock quantities or amounts and references. The Stores area refreshes its visible records periodically while it is open, online and verified, without forcing a full page refresh. Search text, the selected subtab and table scroll position are preserved where possible.

Chapter 15: Weekly Supervision

Weekly Supervision gives the Head Teacher a structured record of weekly teaching checks and provides each linked Teacher with read-only access to their own feedback history.

15.1 Who Can Record or View Checks

The Head Teacher records and edits weekly supervision checks. Teachers cannot change supervision outcomes. A Teacher can view only records linked to that Teacher's Staff record; an account without a Staff link is told to ask the Head Teacher to link it.

The Head Teacher is the reviewer and is excluded from new classroom-staff checks. Existing historical records are preserved even if the staff list or roles later change.

15.2 Recording a Weekly Check

Select the Monday for the week, then choose Record weekly check. A check records:

  • Teaching staff member and class
  • Week beginning, academic year, term and date checked
  • Whether lesson plans were reviewed/marked
  • Lesson-plan outcome: Not checked, Submitted, Not submitted, or Needs revision
  • Lesson-plan comments
  • Whether the attendance register was reviewed
  • Register outcome: Not checked, Up to date, Incomplete, or Not available
  • Attendance-register comments
  • Teacher feedback / action required
  • Optional follow-up date and status: No follow-up, Open, or Completed

The date checked cannot be before the selected week, and a follow-up date cannot be earlier than the check date.

When editing an existing check, the teacher, class, week, academic year and term remain fixed. Create another record if one of those identifying fields must change.

15.3 Subject Checks and Counts

For each subject checked, the Head Teacher can record:

  • Exercises given during the week
  • Assessments given during the week
  • Books checked
  • How many of the checked books were marked
  • Comments for that subject

These numbers are weekly teaching-supervision activity counts, not pupil totals or automatically calculated grades. Counts are whole numbers, and books marked cannot exceed books checked.

Subject choices come from the Subjects tab in its saved order. Repeated subject names are shown once, and a subject already used in another supervision row is unavailable until that row is changed or removed.

15.4 Drafts, Refresh and Export

If the verified workspace and supervision choices were already loaded, the Head Teacher can prepare a supervision entry offline. It is saved as a draft on that device, not as an official school record. Reopen Weekly Supervision and use Resume saved draft or discard it before starting a different check.

Official saving requires internet and a verified Head Teacher account. If confirmation fails, retry the same retained save rather than creating another copy. If the record changed on another device, SchoolHub can require a refresh before the edit is accepted.

Refresh checks loads server-confirmed changes from another device. The Head Teacher can also use Export this week to download the selected week's supervision records as CSV. The weekly summary identifies staff with no recorded check for the selected week.

Chapter 16: Term History

The Term History tab is used to browse older term/year information and export historical results. It includes Term / Academic Year selection, Class selection, historical report generation, CSV export and Start New Term.

Chapter 17: Activity Log

The Activity Log provides a record of important actions performed within the school workspace.

Chapter 18: Notifications & Communication

Notifications/Communication v1 adds a persistent in-app communication layer to SchoolHub. Notifications are stored for the intended user and remain available in the Notification Center until reviewed.

18.1 Notification Center

The notification bell displays an unread badge when new items are waiting. Users can also open Profile → Settings → Notifications.

The Notification Center keeps notification history, distinguishes read and unread items and provides Mark all read. Pending teachers can open Notifications from the Waiting for Approval screen, and disabled teachers can still review their own notification history.

18.2 Automatic Account Notifications

SchoolHub creates notifications for important membership events:

  • Head Teacher: a new teacher joins the school and waits for approval.
  • Head Teacher: an existing teacher explicitly signs in with email/password or Google.
  • Teacher: account approved.
  • Teacher: join request rejected.
  • Teacher: access disabled or reactivated.
  • Teacher: removed from the school.
  • Teacher: class or subject assignments changed.
  • Teacher: a submitted stock request is approved/issued or rejected.
  • Head Teacher: a teacher submits a stock request for review.

Ordinary page refreshes and restored login sessions do not generate a fresh sign-in alert. SchoolHub also suppresses repeated explicit sign-in alerts occurring within a short interval.

A teacher who is still waiting for approval can use Check Approval Status to refresh the membership state after the Head Teacher has acted.

18.3 School Announcements

The Head Teacher can prepare and send a SchoolHub announcement to active teacher accounts. Each announcement is stored in the recipients' Notification Center rather than being a temporary on-screen message only.

18.4 Browser / PWA Notifications

Where the browser supports notifications, the user can optionally grant permission for SchoolHub to show a browser/PWA alert while the application is running or backgrounded.

Current limitation: Notifications/Communication v1 does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging to wake a completely closed PWA. If SchoolHub is fully closed, the persisted notification remains in the user's history and appears when SchoolHub is opened again.

Chapter 19: Fees & Receipts (FIS)

The current Fees & Receipts system has been expanded from the older single-fee model into a multi-category fee-item system. It keeps separate pupil charges, payments, allocations, balances, receipts, adjustments and arrears while preserving legacy v40 fee information during migration.

Financial protection: Financial mutations are protected for the active Head Teacher. Confirmed balances come from the server-backed FIS ledger rather than from unconfirmed local changes.

19.1 Fee Categories and Fee Items

Reusable fee categories make it easier to organise school charges. Examples can include PTA, Feeding, Examination, Sports, Development or any other category the school chooses.

A fee item can be created for a particular academic year and term and can be applied to:

  • one class
  • multiple selected classes
  • all classes
  • one individual pupil

This allows the school to keep several separate fee items in the same term instead of forcing all charges into one class fee.

19.2 Pupil Charges, Payments and Receipts

Each pupil charge keeps separate values for Due, Paid and Balance.

Payments can be allocated automatically to the oldest outstanding debt first. Manual allocation is also available when a payment must be directed to selected charges.

Receipts show how a payment was allocated, helping the school trace which fee items were settled by each payment.

19.3 Discounts, Adjustments and Reversals

SchoolHub supports individual discounts and other adjustments without destroying the original fee record. Safe editing rules prevent a fee from being reduced below money already paid, and paid charges cannot simply be cancelled as though the payment never happened.

Where a payment must be undone, the system supports void/reversal handling so the affected balance can be restored through the financial ledger.

19.4 Financial Dashboard and Reports

The Financial Dashboard summarises the confirmed FIS ledger with indicators such as:

  • Total Expected
  • Total Collected
  • Outstanding
  • Collection Rate
  • Arrears
  • Pupils Owing
  • Discounts
  • Added Adjustments

Reports can be filtered by academic year, term, class, fee category, balance status and production/test records. Reporting views can also group information by category, class, academic period and payment method.

Detailed data can be exported to CSV, and report views can be printed or saved as PDF using the browser's print system.

19.5 Pupil and Parent Fee Statements

A pupil statement brings together the pupil's financial history in one place. It can include:

  • fee items and categories
  • academic periods
  • base charges
  • discounts and additions
  • amount due, paid and outstanding
  • payment receipts
  • payment methods and references
  • void status where applicable

Parent-facing statements use the pupil's existing Guardian Name and Parent Phone details. Previous arrears and current outstanding amounts are shown separately. Future charges are excluded by default unless the user deliberately chooses to include them.

19.6 Arrears Management

Arrears Management helps the Head Teacher separate previous-period arrears from current-term outstanding balances.

Filters are available for class, fee category, guardian/phone search and production/test records. Settled, cancelled, future and test records are excluded from the normal production arrears view by default.

Selected pupils can be prepared for statements or reminders directly from the arrears list.

19.7 Reminder Generator

The Reminder Generator prepares parent fee reminder messages for either one pupil or selected owing pupils.

A reminder can cover:

  • previous arrears only
  • previous arrears plus current-term outstanding balance

An optional deadline and note can be added before generating the reminder.

Available reminder actions include:

  • Preview reminder
  • Copy message - SchoolHub shows a visible confirmation after a successful copy
  • Open WhatsApp - opens the prepared message in the WhatsApp app or browser destination available on the device
  • Print / Save reminder PDF
Important: SchoolHub does not automatically send a WhatsApp message. It prepares the message and opens WhatsApp or the browser destination. The user still decides whether to send it. Parent reminders are based on eligible production balances, not test fee records.

19.8 FIS Pending Saves and Confirmation

FIS writes use a protected pending-save queue. If a financial action has not yet been accepted by the server, it remains pending and is not included in confirmed balances.

A rejected financial queue entry can block later financial saves until it is reviewed. When SchoolHub offers Dismiss rejected entry, that action removes the failed queue item itself; it does not delete an already confirmed payment, receipt or charge.

After connectivity returns, valid queued financial operations can continue synchronising. Use Refresh balances when needed to reload confirmed ledger information.

The general SchoolHub sync indicator and the protected FIS financial queue are related but not identical. A screen can show the main workspace as up to date while an FIS save is still waiting for server confirmation.

Chapter 20: Billing & Credits

SchoolHub contains Billing & Credits infrastructure for report-card generation.

Current release status: Billing remains suspended in the application. The retained payment and report-credit functions are currently test functionality and should not be interpreted as active live billing.

Chapter 21: Offline Mode, Drafts & Synchronisation

A previously verified signed-in account on the same device can reopen locally stored school information when Firebase is temporarily unavailable. First sign-in, joining a school and loading an uncached workspace on a new device still require internet.

21.1 Saved locally / pending

Supported pupil edits, offline pupil additions, grades, pupil attendance and remarks can be saved locally before upload. Teachers remain restricted to their assigned classes and subjects. A pending record change stays in SchoolHub's durable local queue until the account is revalidated and the cloud accepts it.

21.2 Device-scoped form drafts

A draft is different from a pending record save. It is a form saved on the current device that still needs a deliberate submit action later. Current examples include My Details, teacher stock requests, Add Staff, and Weekly Supervision.

Drafts are scoped to the relevant account and school and are not automatically shared to another device. Do not clear site/app data while a draft is needed for recovery.

21.3 Reconnecting and Background Sync

When the internet returns, SchoolHub may show Waiting to reconnect → Checking → Up to date while it verifies the account and cloud connection. It retries recovery while the app is open, sends supported pending edits, acknowledges successful writes and then refreshes cloud data.

The PWA uses native Background Sync where the browser supports it. Supported queued record changes may sometimes upload after the app window closes, but timing is not guaranteed. Reopen SchoolHub with internet and wait for confirmation if a save remains pending. Signing out stops background uploads.

Important distinction: My Details drafts, stock-request drafts, Add Staff drafts and Weekly Supervision drafts are not automatically submitted merely because the header shows Up to date. A server-confirmed action must complete through its own workflow.

21.4 Cross-device updates

An open Classes list can receive supported class additions, renames and confirmed deletions automatically from another device. The Stores section periodically refreshes its own visible records while online and verified. Other lists may still need an explicit refresh on the observing device.

21.5 Service-worker updates

The current PWA manages service-worker registration, update checks and lifecycle events and surfaces registration problems through diagnostics rather than silently ignoring them.

Chapter 22: Data Protection & Recovery

The current SchoolHub safety architecture includes local recovery snapshots, a durable local save journal, persistent pending-sync information, scoped device drafts, protection against incomplete cloud hydration, explicit deletion tracking, server-side validation, idempotent protected operations, revision/conflict checks, selected recovery tools and rollover archives.

Even with these safeguards, regular exported backups remain strongly recommended.

Chapter 24: Installing SchoolHub as an App

Because SchoolHub is a Progressive Web App, supported browsers can install it without requiring a traditional app-store package. When available, use Profile → Help → Install SchoolHub.

After installation, SchoolHub continues using the same school account and cloud data. The service worker provides the app shell and supports offline-first reopening where the required data has already been verified and cached.

Chapter 25: Mobile Tables and Horizontal Scrolling

Wide SchoolHub tables are designed to remain usable on phones. Where a table contains more columns than can fit on the screen, swipe left or right inside the table to scroll horizontally.

Fees & Receipts tables pin their main row-label column; the Arrears table keeps the pupil/name area beside its selection checkbox. Stores tables keep headings aligned and the first identifying column visible where the layout supports it. Weekly Supervision also uses a sideways-scrollable table so teacher/class context remains readable while reviewing check columns.

The page itself continues to scroll vertically as normal. Horizontal scrolling applies only to the wide table area.

Chapter 28: Conclusion

AlatiphA SchoolHub has developed from a simple school-record system into a broader school-management platform covering the daily workflow of Head Teachers and Teachers.

Its role-based accounts separate administrative responsibilities from classroom access. Staff, Classes, Students, Subjects, Attendance, Grades, Remarks, Reports, Stores/Assets/Liabilities, Weekly Supervision, Fees & Receipts and Term History provide the main working environment, while notifications, announcements, account security, financial reporting, parent statements, reminders, backups, cloud synchronisation, offline support and year-end rollover support the wider life of school records.

The current v40 safety architecture places particular emphasis on protecting school data and account identity by preserving pending edits and scoped drafts, controlling sensitive cloud operations, preventing duplicate teacher memberships for the same normalised email, isolating protected financial/stock/supervision saves, applying conflict checks, maintaining recovery information and reducing the risk of incomplete local data overwriting fuller cloud records.

For schools using SchoolHub, the best practice remains simple: keep records current, use one teacher identity per person, review join requests carefully, supervise teaching consistently, record stock and liabilities with references, distinguish drafts from confirmed saves, monitor notifications and synchronisation, use role permissions responsibly, and maintain regular backups.
