A Sync Pending indicator means supported local changes are waiting to be sent. Do not assume a pending change has reached the cloud until synchronization succeeds.

Review all Promote, Repeat, Graduate/Complete and Transfer/Leave decisions before applying them. Emergency Restore is intended for a valid SchoolHub rollover backup and should be used carefully.
